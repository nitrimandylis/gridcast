HOW IT WORKS

01

Clean laps

Every 2026 race lap, minus in-laps, out-laps, the first two racing laps and anything under yellow. Nothing from before the 2026 regulation change touches car pace: those cars no longer exist.

02

Direct model

Plackett-Luce: each driver gets a strength from grid position and season pace, the winner is drawn by softmax, removed, and the next place drawn again. Fit by maximum likelihood on finishing orders. Two weights, nothing to hide behind.

03

Simulator

Ten thousand races, lap by lap: a form draw per driver, tyre degradation fit on 11,000 laps, a minimum-race-time pit strategy search, safety cars and red flags at each circuit's historic rate, retirements by team. Cars do not interact, on purpose.

04

Score in public

Ranked probability score on every driver's full position distribution, lower is better, always next to the baseline “you finish where you start”. Thursday calls run on a guessed grid, Saturday calls on the real one, and the commit hash is the timestamp.

Every forecast here existed before the race it describes.
Git history says when. The scorecard says how wrong.
